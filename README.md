# Snatchole — Copy. Tap. Save.

Snatchole is a native Android app that saves videos and their sound to your phone in one tap.
Copy a link, open Snatchole (or tap its home-screen widget), and the file lands in your gallery.

**[⬇ Download the latest APK](https://github.com/huckskr/snatchole-releases/releases/latest)**

## Features

- **One-tap saving** from TikTok, Instagram, X, Facebook, Reddit, Pinterest and more, in the best available quality
- **Music mode**: save just the sound of any video
- **Home-screen widgets**: one tap saves the copied link as video or music
- **Built-in gallery** to watch, share and manage everything you saved
- **Fast**: large files download in parallel ranges, up to 2.4× faster than a single stream
- **24 languages**: the app opens in your phone's language
- **Light**: a 7.4 MB APK

## Install

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/huckskr/snatchole-releases/releases/latest) on your Android phone.
2. Download `Snatchole-vX.Y.Z-release.apk` and open it.
3. If asked, allow installing apps from your browser, then tap **Install**.
